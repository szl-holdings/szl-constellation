# SZL Design System — One-of-One Vertical Identities — 2026-09-04

**Directive:** owner — every vertical fully operational, each front end unique,
elegant, holographic; notes from killinchu, a-11-oy.com, hatun, and the category
leaders; then throw our style in and beat them.

## Global doctrine (every surface)

- Dark holographic base (#04070d), bloom-lit accents, space-mono for evidence
- Honesty states are visible design elements, not footnotes: MEASURED teal,
  DECLARED blue, UNAVAILABLE dim, METHOD amber, BLOCKED red-dim
- No metric without its receipt; no green that wasn't computed
- 44px targets, visible focus, reduced-motion and forced-colors contracts
